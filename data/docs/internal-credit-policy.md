# Internal Credit Underwriting Policy

## FOIR limit
The fixed obligation to income ratio, FOIR, must not exceed 50 percent of net monthly income for salaried applicants and 45 percent for self-employed applicants. Existing EMIs and the proposed EMI are both counted in obligations.

## Credit score bands
A credit score of 750 and above is prime and eligible for the standard rate. A score of 700 to 749 is near prime and priced 0.50 percent higher. A score below 700 must be declined unless a senior credit officer approves an exception in writing.

## Delegation of authority
Branch credit officers may sanction home loans up to 75 lakh. Loans above 75 lakh and up to 2 crore need regional credit head approval. Loans above 2 crore go to the central credit committee.

## Exceptions
Policy exceptions must be recorded in the exception register with the reason, the approver and the compensating factors. Exceptions on more than 5 percent of a branch's monthly sanctions trigger an audit review.
