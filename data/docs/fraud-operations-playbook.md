# Fraud Operations Playbook

## Suspicious transaction escalation
Any single transaction above 10 lakh with no prior pattern must be escalated to the fraud desk within 2 hours. The fraud desk may place a temporary hold of up to 24 hours while contacting the customer.

## Reporting to the regulator
Confirmed fraud cases above 1 lakh must be reported to the compliance team within 24 hours, and the compliance team files the regulatory report within 7 days.

## Mule account indicators
Indicators include many small credits followed by a single large debit, a new account with immediate high turnover, and multiple accounts sharing one device or phone number.
