# Home Loan Product Guide

## Eligibility
Applicants must be between 21 and 65 years of age at loan maturity. Salaried applicants need at least 2 years of total work experience and 1 year with the current employer. Self-employed applicants need at least 3 years of business continuity. A minimum credit score of 700 is required for approval.

## Loan amount and tenure
The maximum home loan tenure is 30 years (360 months). The loan can finance up to 80 percent of the property value for loans up to 30 lakh, and up to 75 percent for loans above 30 lakh. The borrower pays the remaining margin money from own funds.

## Interest rate
The indicative floating interest rate is 10.5 percent per annum linked to the bank's external benchmark rate. The rate is reset every quarter. Women co-applicants receive a concession of 0.05 percent on the spread.

## Processing fee
The processing fee is 0.50 percent of the sanctioned loan amount, subject to a minimum of 5,000 rupees and a maximum of 25,000 rupees, plus applicable taxes. The fee is non-refundable once the loan is sanctioned.

## Documents required
Identity proof, address proof, last 3 months salary slips, last 6 months bank statements, Form 16 or income tax returns for the last 2 years, and property documents including the sale agreement and title deed.
