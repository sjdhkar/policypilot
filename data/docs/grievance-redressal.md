# Grievance Redressal Policy

## Levels of escalation
Level 1 is the branch or the customer care helpline, which must acknowledge within 24 hours and resolve within 7 working days. Level 2 is the Nodal Officer, who must respond within 10 working days. Level 3 is the Principal Nodal Officer, who must respond within 15 working days.

## External escalation
If the complaint is not resolved within 30 days, the customer may approach the banking ombudsman. The bank must display the ombudsman details at every branch.
