# Card Disputes and Chargebacks

## Reporting unauthorised transactions
Customers must report an unauthorised card transaction within 3 working days of receiving the alert to have zero liability. If reported between 4 and 7 working days, liability is limited to 10,000 rupees. After 7 working days the liability is decided by the board approved policy.

## Dispute timeline
The bank acknowledges a dispute within 2 working days and resolves it within 90 days. A provisional credit is given within 10 working days for unauthorised transactions reported on time.

## Blocking a card
A lost or stolen card can be blocked immediately through the mobile app, net banking, or the 24x7 helpline. A blocked card cannot be reactivated, and a replacement card is issued within 7 working days.
