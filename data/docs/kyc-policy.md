# KYC and Account Opening Policy

## Documents accepted
Officially valid documents include passport, driving licence, voter identity card, and the national identity number card. Proof of address can be a recent utility bill not older than 2 months or a rental agreement.

## Periodic KYC refresh
Customers must refresh KYC every 2 years for high risk accounts, every 8 years for medium risk accounts, and every 10 years for low risk accounts. Accounts with an overdue refresh may be restricted to credit transactions only.

## Video KYC
Video KYC is available for new savings accounts. The customer must be in India, hold an original identity document, and complete a live liveness check with a bank official.

## Minimum balance
The minimum average monthly balance for a regular savings account is 5,000 rupees in metro branches and 2,000 rupees in rural branches.
