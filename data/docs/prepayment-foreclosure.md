# Prepayment and Foreclosure Policy

## Floating rate loans
No prepayment or foreclosure charges apply to floating rate home loans taken by individual borrowers. Part prepayment is allowed at any time, with a minimum part prepayment amount equal to one EMI.

## Fixed rate loans
For fixed rate loans, a prepayment charge of 2 percent of the principal outstanding applies on the amount prepaid. The charge is waived if prepayment is made from the borrower's own savings after the first 12 months.

## Foreclosure process
To foreclose a loan, the borrower submits a written request. The bank issues a foreclosure statement valid for 7 days. After full payment, the bank releases the original property documents within 15 working days, and the lien is removed from the register.

## Effect of part prepayment
After a part prepayment the borrower can choose to either reduce the EMI or reduce the tenure. Reducing the tenure saves more interest overall.
